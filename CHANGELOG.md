# Changelog

## 0.1.6

- You decide how much is checked: take my word (where it starts), check the
  key stories, or check everything.
- A named person is shown as "named, not contacted" until they agree to
  confirm. The top band, Proven, needs a document, a public source, or a
  person who has agreed.
- Every resume draft and interview guide ends with "Check these words".
- In a fit check, a loose match rises only when you say what you did. Matched
  and proven requirements are counted separately.
- Each step is split into parts, with a clear stop between them.
- One question per turn. One question about confidential figures per card.
- Answers are saved as they are given. A short bank index lets a later
  conversation pick up where you stopped.
- The story map shows whether your own part in a result is backed, separately
  from the result.
- The rules shared by all four steps now travel with each step.
- New graphics and a reorganized README.

## 0.1.5

- Tappable choices for questions with a fixed set of answers.
- Story cards shown as cards, with a progress line during a session.
- An optional Story Bank spreadsheet.
- "What you need" added to the README.

## 0.1.4

- Depth for each story: Quick, Standard or Deep.
- A strength score and band for each story, with a "strengthen next" list.

## 0.1.3

- A figure whose clearance is unknown appears only in drafts, under "Confirm
  before sending".
- One Core Reel per target job type.
- "Your data" section added to the README.

## 0.1.2

- Validation based on what proves the result, with the user's role recorded
  separately.
- Approved uses for each story: resume, interview, post or book.

## 0.1.1

- Fewer questions per session.
- "Approved" and "validated" recorded as two separate things.

## 0.1.0

- First version: four steps, story cards, story map, fit check and highlight
  reels.

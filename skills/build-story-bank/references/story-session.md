# The story session

A guided pass of about 15 minutes per story. The user reacts to a draft rather
than telling the story from scratch. Ask one question at a time and wait for
the answer.

## 1. Show the draft (1 minute)

Present the card with its empty fields marked. Ask the user to correct anything
wrong in the filled fields first.

## 2. Fill the gaps (8 to 10 minutes)

Ask one question per empty field, in this order: Result, Role and scope,
Actions, Obstacles, Evidence, Lessons. Use the follow-up when an answer is
vague.

| Empty field | Question | Follow-up if the answer is vague |
|---|---|---|
| Result | What changed, measured how, from what baseline, over what period? | If you had to defend the number to a CFO, what would you show? |
| Role and scope | What was your title, who reported to you, and what did you own (P&L, budget, geography)? | Who did you need approval from? |
| Actions | What are the three things you personally did that made the difference? | What would not have happened if you had been absent? |
| Obstacles | What was the hardest part, and what did you do about it? | Give one specific conversation or decision. |
| Evidence | Who could back this up, and is there a document or number you could point to? | A former boss, a customer, a board deck? |
| Lessons | What would you repeat, and what would you do differently? | How did you apply this later? |

## 3. Challenge (2 minutes)

Ask two tough questions a senior interviewer would ask:

1. "What share of this was you versus the team?"
2. "What would a skeptic say about that number?"

Record the answers in Evidence and Obstacles. If an answer weakens a claim,
rewrite the claim to the weaker, accurate version.

## 4. Approve (1 minute)

Read the finished card back. Ask the user to approve it and to tick its
approved uses. Then apply the validation rule: mark the card `validated` only
when it has proof from a document, a confirmed public source, or a named
person. Otherwise mark it `unvalidated` and say what proof is missing.

## Between sessions

The user may answer open questions later by text or voice memo. Add the answers
to the card and update its completeness count.
